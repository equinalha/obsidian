Now, to fix your rounded upper back and forward shoulders, you'll need to do lots of pulling exercises.

- Barbell rows
- Dumbbell rows
- Machine rows
- Bodyweight rows

But if you're just going to do one, I would do facepulls 👇👇

> [!note] 📌
> **Rob - Nothing Barred Fitness **[***@Rob_NBF:***](https://www.twitter.com/Rob_NBF)
Facepulls are NOT an ego exercise.

You do not want much weight here at all.

Go slowly, really squeeze the contraction at the end of the movement.

Go for high reps, 15-20 reps.

Stop when you can't get another rep with good form.

> [!note] 📌
> **Rob - Nothing Barred Fitness **[***@Rob_NBF:***](https://www.twitter.com/Rob_NBF)
You can also do facepulls at home with a resistance band. Just anchor it somewhere at about head height.

Add in band pull aparts to improve your shoulder posture.
