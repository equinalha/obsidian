## How does it work?

After you complete the connection, you can save any tweet by mentioning [**@SaveToNotion**](https://twitter.com/SaveToNotion) in a reply, you must use one of these hashtags: **#tweet** or **#thread**, otherwise, the bot won't save that tweet.

## How to deal with this database?

There are some things you can't change in this database, to avoid facing errors, You **shouldn't **delete or rename any property of those:

![[tweet_prps.png|You can hide and reorder any property, but never change its name or delete it]]

### But you can add new properties and assign any value to them, and you can rename the database, change its cover and icon, move it to another page, share it with others, and duplicate it, you can also add, edit and delete any page in the database.
