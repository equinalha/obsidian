The top 10 body language mistakes 99% of men make (and how to fix them:

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
1) Checking your phone

When you’re in a group and you receive a call/text, what’s your reaction?

Most men grab their phone to answer or reply immediately.

What kind of image do you think that gives off?

You look disinterested in those you’re with.

Reply later.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
2) Your walk

Gents, stop taking small strides.

It makes you seem withdrawn and uncertain.

Do this:

• Stand up straight
• Shoulders back
• Big strides
• Chin up

Confidence starts with your body language.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
3) Eye contact

People won’t trust you if you can’t handle eye contact.

Too little makes you look weak.

Too much makes people feel weirded out.

If you struggle with this, follow the 3 second rule:

3 seconds of eye contact.

Look away.

Repeat.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
4) Personal space

Guys, you might not realize, but you could be a space invader.

Intruding on someone else’s space is disrespecting them.

Respect personal space by staying an arm’s length away.

Move in closer only when you believe you’re welcome.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
5) Limp handshake

Picture this:

You got the job interview. 

You walk in to the building.

Head up, shoulders back, feeling confident.

The interviewer likes you.

Then you give them a limp handshake.

Game over.

Gents, make it firm but not tight.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
6) Hug?

What do you look like if you’re having to ask a girl for a hug?

And what if you stink?

Let her come to you. 

This shows she is the one that has to approach you if anything is going to happen.

Don’t appear needy.

Speaking of foul smells...

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
7) Bad breath

There’s little else more repulsive.

Floss.

Brush your teeth.

Carry mints (or gum).

Bad breath means a bad time with other people.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
8) Fight

Fighting is for dogs who can’t control themselves.

The most attractive thing you can do with the heat turns up is to be disinterested.

Be the epitome of calm and collected.

> [!note] 📌
> **Stirling Cooper **[***@StirlingWisdom:***](https://www.twitter.com/StirlingWisdom)
9) Sitting

Slouching and hunching while walking are signs of fear and uncertainty.

Why would it be different while sitting?

Don’t be afraid to spread out.

It shows you’re relaxed and unafraid to take up space.

Don’t cross your legs or arms - these are unconfident positions.
