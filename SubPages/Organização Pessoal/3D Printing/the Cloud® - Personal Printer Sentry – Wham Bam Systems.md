## Product Details

**Due to shipping limitations, the Cloud® is only available to ship to the US destinations but check with our **[** resellers**](https://www.whambamsystems.com/pages/resellers)** to see if it is available locally.**

The Cloud® is a personal extinguishing device for any 3d printer, laser, or CNC that gives you peace of mind when leaving your machines unattended for hours!

The Cloud® has a hook patch on the rear side and a matching separate loop patch with a strong 3M backing. You can mount this loop patch to a wall, your printer, or to a 3D printed bracket to mount to your printer in an optimal position. Plus our most recent HotBox and HotBox Mega have a loop patch already sewn into the inner top panel to give a convenient mounting point!

Rated as a Class A, B, C, E, and F fire extinguisher, the Cloud® will only activate with direct contact with a flame for at least 3 seconds and will not trigger due to ambient temperature or smoke. Once activated, it makes a brief explosion, launching a cloud of ABC extinguishing powder around the device. The powder quickly extinguishes almost any fire within its perimeter.
